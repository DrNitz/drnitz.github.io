# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a personal portfolio and a small collaboration space. Be decent to other people, and assume good faith.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests and discussions. Disagree about code, not about people.
- Give constructive feedback. Explain *why* a change is suggested.
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions; that is not a personal judgement.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal contact details, patient information or other people's private data in issues or commits.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone else's private information without consent.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, commits) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to drnitz@proton.me. Reports will be reviewed in confidence. Depending on severity, consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from participating. Maintainers may remove or edit comments and commits that violate these standards.
