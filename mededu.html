Notes you can actually use

03 / About

Built in public, still in progress

MedEdu is a personal initiative, not a finished course. Notes are being compiled and reviewed gradually. If a topic is missing, an explanation is unclear, or you spot something that needs correcting, the fastest way to help is a short email.
