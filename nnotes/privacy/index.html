Privacy Policy • Section 1

1. Privacy Philosophy & Local-First Design

Engineered by Harshita (Lead Developer) and Dr.N (Co-Developer), Nnotes is built around a strict local-first model under the motto Cure. Code. Create. Your handwritten notes, sketches, diagrams, voice recordings, and files stay on your physical device.

Zero Cloud Telemetry & No Account Mandatory: We do not operate remote servers that collect, store, or profile your note content. Nnotes requires no user registration, no email signup, and no personal account to create, edit, and organize notes. Note creation and editing operate 100% offline.

Both free and Pro tiers share the exact same core privacy standard: your personal thoughts and documents never leave your phone or tablet without your direct manual action (such as exporting a PDF or sharing a file).

Privacy Policy • Section 2

2. Data Collection, Advertising & Play Data Safety

Nnotes is free to download. To fund ongoing development, caffeine, and maintenance, the free tier displays ads powered by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDKs process telemetry under Google's Privacy Policy. This section matches our Google Play Console Data Safety declaration:

  • Ad delivery & diagnostics: Google's ad SDKs may process device identifiers, IP addresses, general non-precise location (network level), and the Android Advertising ID to serve ads, measure impressions, prevent click fraud, and diagnose network crashes.
  • Complete isolation from note content: Ad SDKs run in a separate sandboxed layer and never have access to your inking canvas, handwritten notes, voice notes, PDFs, or files opened through the Storage Access Framework.
  • No Sale of Data: The developers do not sell, rent, broker, or trade personal data or note content with third parties.

Privacy Policy • Section 4

4. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Choices

  • Notes and documents: Stored locally in your device's app storage or chosen SAF folder. Deleting a note inside Nnotes permanently removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app clears all local sandbox data. We retain zero remote backups.
  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: In Android Ads Settings, toggle "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization". AdMob will serve only contextual, non-personalized ads.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Subscribing to Nnotes Pro completely disables Google AdMob SDK initialization, providing a zero-ad workspace.
Privacy Policy • Section 5

5. On-Device AI: Handwriting Auto-Beautify & Whisper Audio Transcription

Nnotes provides advanced on-device artificial intelligence features that respect your privacy by architecture:

Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Required solely when you actively tap the Record button to capture a voice note. Audio is never recorded in the background or when the app is minimized.
  • On-Device Whisper Speech-to-Text: Audio transcription is powered by on-device Whisper models running natively on your hardware (CPU/NPU). Audio samples are processed strictly in local RAM and are never uploaded to external cloud AI APIs, remote speech servers, or third parties.
  • Handwriting Auto-Beautify: Real-time handwriting recognition and stroke beautification for 80+ world languages run entirely on-device via local ML Kit models. Vector ink data never leaves your device.
  • Smart Math Calculation: Ink equation solving and math calculations are evaluated locally using on-device math parsers with zero network transmission.
Privacy Policy • Section 6

6. Local File Storage, Storage Access Framework & System Pickers

In strict compliance with Google Play's Photo and Video Permissions Policy and modern Android scoped storage:

  • Zero Broad Media Permissions: Nnotes does not declare or request broad READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or general shared storage permissions.
  • System Document & Photo Pickers: When inserting an image onto a note, opening a PDF textbook, or selecting a local video file for the floating picture-in-picture player, Nnotes launches Android's native system file picker (ActivityResultContracts.OpenDocument / GetContent). Access is strictly limited to the individual file deliberately chosen by the user.
  • Direct Document Provider: Nnotes implements a secure DocumentsProvider allowing you to save notes directly to a folder of your choice on your device or SD card.
Privacy Policy • Section 7

7. Screen Recording, MediaProjection & Live Streaming

Nnotes includes a built-in lecture presentation, screen recording, and live-streaming tool:

  • Explicit User Consent: Screen recording utilizes Android's MediaProjection API (service type: mediaProjection). Recording can only be started by deliberate user interaction, which triggers the mandatory Android system prompt ("Start recording or casting?").
  • Local Video Output: Captured video and internal audio are encoded directly into MP4 files stored locally in your device's Movies/Nnotes/ directory.
  • Zero-Cost Live Streaming: When streaming, the video feed connects directly to your chosen streaming server or audience QR code endpoint via standard RTMP/WebRTC protocols. The developers operate no intermediate surveillance servers.
