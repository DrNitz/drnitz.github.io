Core Features & USPs

Smart Auto-Beautify

Write naturally with stylus or finger, then convert handwriting and rough shapes into crisp digital text and vectors using on-device ML Kit recognition — with multi-language support and no cloud upload.

Limitless Infinite Canvas

Break free from page restrictions. Pan and pinch-zoom endlessly across a limitless canvas with customizable Dot, Ruled, Graph, and Plain background grids.

PDF Annotation & Planner

Import, read and annotate PDFs, and organize notebooks with folders. Fast vector rendering keeps zooming and panning smooth even on large multi-page documents.

Export & Share Freely

Export notes as crisp vector PDFs or high-resolution PNGs, and share a whole notebook or a single page in one tap.

Glassmorphism + 3D Clay UI

A glassmorphism theme with soft 3D clay elements, vibrant accents and rounded selection boxes. Toolbars are fully customizable — floating or docked.

Floating Drag & Drop Palette

A customizable floating toolbar that stays out of your way. Move it anywhere on screen, long-press to reorder tools, or switch between vertical, horizontal, and split layouts.

7 Animated Companions

Choose from Student, Doctor, Teacher, Engineer, Designer, Lawyer or the N-notes Mascot — animated companions that hang out on canvas or toolbar with motivating quotes.

Local-First Notes

No account needed: notes are managed on your device with Android's Storage Access Framework and recognition runs on-device. The app is ad-supported, so ad providers may process advertising identifiers — see Privacy & Terms for details.
