Zero Cloud Transmissions

On-Device Whisper Speech-to-Text

Record lectures and dictate notes right into the canvas. Transcription runs on your tablet's local processor using whisper.cpp.

No Audio Leaves Your Device

Most speech apps send recordings to remote server farms. Nnotes packages an optimized local Whisper model running on Android's native CPU/GPU runtime.

  • 0 bytes sent over network: works in airplane mode.
  • Instant markdown nodes: text inserts directly onto canvas.
  • No time limits: record study sessions without subscriptions.

Study Tools for High-Volume Learning

Study Tape (Cloze Recall)

Lay strips of opaque study tape over handwritten formulas or textbook terms. Tap to peel and check your answer; tap again to re-mask. Active recall without making separate flashcards.
