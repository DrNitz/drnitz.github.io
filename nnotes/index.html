Crafted with a relentless focus on speed, total privacy, and tactile stylus ergonomics.

FLAGSHIP

On-Device Whisper Voice Notes

Capture thoughts, lectures, and clinical rounds with speech-to-text powered directly on your device by whisper.cpp. Zero audio leaves your phone or tablet. No subscriptions, no cloud latency, and 100% offline transcription rendered directly into rich markdown canvas notes.

✓ Whisper Engine: whisper.cpp (offline)
• Audio Stream: 16kHz PCM (local)
• Transcription Latency: ~180ms
• Cloud Network Request: NONE (0 bytes)
80+ LANGUAGES

Smart Auto-Beautify

Write freely with finger or stylus. One tap transforms messy handwriting into crisp, formatted digital typography and snaps rough sketches into clean geometric vectors using local ML Kit models.

NEW

Mind Maps & Idea Trees

Structure complex thoughts visually with collapsible branch nodes, rich paragraph content, customizable node colors and shapes, and embedded image attachments directly linked to your concept tree.

ACTIVE RECALL

Study Tape & Medical Plates

Apply cloze masking tape over handwritten lecture notes or imported PDFs to test your recall. Includes direct access to the famous Stanford Bassett medical anatomical dissection plates for clinical study.

HD BROADCAST

Screen Recording & Live Stream

Capture high-definition canvas tutorials locally as MP4 video, or share your whiteboard live with students or peers via zero-cost Audience QR Code streaming and RTMP endpoints.

Limitless Infinite Canvas

Break free from page margins. Pan and zoom seamlessly from 10% to 5000% across an infinite vector canvas. Switch between Dot, Ruled, Isometric, Engineering Graph, and Plain backgrounds.

PDF Annotation & Planners

Import heavy textbooks, research papers, and slide decks. Write annotations with sub-pixel vector accuracy, insert sticky notes, reorganize pages, and export back as crisp vector documents.

10 CHARACTERS

10 Animated Companions

Meet Doki, Lesso, Torque, Vector, Clause, Inky, Shelly, Serena, Mochi, and Beacon. Interactive 3D study mascots that live on your toolbar to provide focus tips and motivational bursts.

PRIVACY FIRST

100% Local-First & Safe

No accounts, no logins, no personal tracking. Your notebooks are stored securely on your device via Android Storage Access Framework (SAF). Zero telemetry on note content.
