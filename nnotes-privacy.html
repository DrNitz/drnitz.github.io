Privacy Policy • Section 1

1. Privacy Philosophy & Local-First Design

Engineered by Harshita (Lead Developer) and Dr.N (Co-Developer), Nnotes is built around a local-first model under the motto Cure. Code. Create. Your handwritten notes, diagrams, and ideas stay on your device. This page explains both that local-first design and the ad-supported parts of the app honestly.

🔒
No Note Collection & No Account Requirement: We do not collect or transmit your note content. Note-taking works without an internet connection, and there is no user registration or email sign-up. The app is ad-supported; Google's ad SDKs process advertising identifiers as described below in Section 2.

Handwriting recognition runs on your device using on-device ML Kit; note content is never uploaded. "Offline" here refers to note creation and recognition — ad delivery and measurement require a connection and are handled by Google's ad SDKs under their own policies.

Privacy Policy • Section 2

2. Data Collection, Ads & Play Data Safety

Your notes and drawings stay on your device. Nnotes is ad-supported: it integrates Google AdMob and AdSense SDKs to keep the app free. Those SDKs process advertising-related data under Google's Privacy Policy. This section matches the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration.

  • Ad delivery and measurement: Google's ad SDKs may process device identifiers, IP addresses, generalized location data and the Android Advertising ID to select ads, measure campaigns and — where your settings and region allow — personalize advertising.
  • Strict separation from note content: Advertising SDKs do not receive access to your handwritten notes, canvas data, or files saved through the Storage Access Framework. We do not sell or upload your note content.
  • Developer vs. service providers: The developer does not sell or share note data with third parties. Ad providers process ad-related data as independent services under their own policies. That is separate from your notes, which are never shared.

What the Play Data Safety listing declares

Data type (as declared on Google Play)Why it is processed
App activityAd delivery and measurement (for example, interactions with ads) and basic app performance diagnostics.
App info and performanceCrash and performance diagnostics so the app can be kept stable and improved.
Device or other IDsAdvertising identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, used by Google's ad SDKs for ad selection, personalization (where permitted) and measurement.

Encryption in transit: data handled by these services is encrypted in transit. Data can't be deleted by us: the developer cannot delete ad or technical identifiers held by Google. Your note content is different — it lives only on your device and is deleted there (see Section 4). Identifiers controlled by Google can be reset or opted out through Android settings.

Privacy Policy • Section 3

3. Children

Nnotes is rated for ages 3+ on Google Play, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details in Section 11 and we will address it.

Where required for child-directed audiences, non-personalized ads are used instead of personalized ads. Parents or guardians can contact drnitz@proton.me with privacy questions or requests concerning a child.

Privacy Policy • Section 4

4. Data Retention, Deletion & Your Choices

  • Notes and drawings: stored only on your device. Delete them inside the app, or remove the app (and its app data) to clear them from the device. We hold no copy to retain or delete.
  • Ad and technical identifiers: retained and processed by Google under its policies, not by the developer. As stated in the Play Data Safety listing, this data can't be deleted by us.
  • Reset your Android Advertising ID: open Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Google Settings → Ads) and choose to reset the advertising ID.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: in the same Android ads settings, enable Delete advertising ID or turn off ad personalization. You can also control ad settings in your Google account.
  • Requests and questions: email drnitz@proton.me. We can help with questions about this policy and with locally stored note data, but cannot delete identifiers held by Google.
Privacy Policy • Section 5

5. On-Device Auto-Beautify Engine

Nnotes' flagship Magical Auto-Beautify feature converts messy handwriting into styled digital text:

  • 100% On-Device Parsing: All stroke analysis and handwriting-to-text recognition are performed locally on your device CPU/GPU.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Your handwritten text is never sent to external AI servers or remote recognition APIs.
Privacy Policy • Section 6

6. Local File Storage & Storage Access Framework (SAF)

Nnotes integrates with Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF):

  • No Broad File Access Needed: Nnotes does not ask for broad READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions to access your photos or files.
  • Isolated Directory Access: Files are read and written strictly in folders you grant access to via the system document picker.
Privacy Policy • Section 7

7. 3D Companion Mascots & Local Assets

Our interactive 3D profession companions (Doctor, Student, Engineer, Artist) are bundled locally within the app installation package. No external servers or network connections are established when choosing or interacting with your companion.
